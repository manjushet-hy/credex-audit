# Economics: The Value of the Audit

## 1. Converted Lead Value (CLV)
- **Revenue Model:** Credex earns a commission/spread on discounted credits (e.g., Cursor, Claude).
- **Average Deal:** A startup switching to a Credex-managed plan for 20 users might save $5,000/year. Credex earns ~$1,000–$1,500 in margin.
- **CLV:** Estimated at **$1,250 per converted lead.**

## 2. CAC at Scale
- **Organic (GTM Plan):** ~$5 (Hosting + API costs for the audit).
- **Social/Referral:** ~$15 (Cost of community management and outreach).
- **Inbound Content:** ~$40 (Cost of high-quality "AI Spend" content).

## 3. Conversion Funnel Math
- **Audit Completed → Lead Captured:** 20% (Gate required to see full results).
- **Lead Captured → Consultation Booked:** 5% (Based on high-savings alerts).
- **Consultation → Credit Purchase:** 20%.
- **Overall Conversion:** ~0.2% of visitors.
- **Profitability:** If CLV is $1,250, we can afford a blended CAC of up to $250. Our current organic plan is well within this margin.

## 4. The Path to $1M ARR
To drive $1M in ARR (Margin) in 18 months:
- We need ~800 customers/year (~65/month).
- At a 0.2% visitor-to-customer rate, we need **32,500 monthly visitors.**
- This is achievable by establishing the tool as the "Defacto Standard" for AI spend auditing, similar to how "CloudHealth" or "Vantage" work for AWS.
