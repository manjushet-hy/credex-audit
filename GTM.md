# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: Credex AI Spend Auditor

## 1. The Target User
**The Fractional CFO or Operations Manager at Series A/B Startups.**
Specifically: Companies with 20–100 employees who have recently seen their "Software/Cloud" line item on the P&L spike due to fragmented AI adoption (engineers on Cursor, marketing on ChatGPT Plus, legal on Claude).

## 2. The "Pre-Intent" Moment
What they are doing right before they need this:
- Reviewing the monthly credit card statement and seeing 15 different $20–$40 subscriptions.
- Preparing for a board meeting where "Efficient Growth" is the theme.
- Searching for *"How to manage team seats on Cursor"* or *"ChatGPT Enterprise vs Team plan."*

## 3. Where They Hang Out
- **Subreddits:** r/startups, r/FractionalCFO, r/SaaS.
- **Slack/Discord:** Pavilion, SaaS School, Latka's SaaS group.
- **X Lists:** Lists of Fintech/Ops influencers.

## 4. The First 100 Users ($0 Budget)
- **Direct Outreach (The "CFO Gift"):** Cold-messaging fractional CFOs on LinkedIn/X offering a free "AI Audit Report" they can use to look like heroes to their clients.
- **The "Tool Roast":** Posting on X/LinkedIn: *"Your startup is likely overpaying for Cursor. Send me your seat count and I'll run a free audit."* 
- **Product Hunt Launch:** A dedicated "Free Tool" launch focused on the "Mint for AI" value proposition.
- **Directory Submission:** Listing on "Free Startup Tools" directories.

## 5. The Unfair Channel
**Credex's Existing Network.**
Credex already has relationships with companies buying discounted credits. This tool acts as the "Top of Funnel" (ToF). We don't just sell credits; we provide the diagnostic that proves you *need* them. 

## 6. Week-1 Traction Goals
- 500 Unique Visits.
- 50 Completed Audits.
- 10 Qualified Leads (booked consultations or high-value saved audits).
- 5 Shared Reports on social media (The Viral Loop).
